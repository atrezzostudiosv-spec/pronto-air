Air Conditioning Maintenance Tips

Simple maintenance tips for residential air conditioning systems.

01

Check Your Filters

Check air conditioning filters monthly.

02

Keep Ductwork Clear

Make sure stored items in attics, garages, or other areas are not restricting airflow through ductwork.

03

Clear Outdoor Equipment

Keep outdoor units clear of grass, leaves, and other debris.
