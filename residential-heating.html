Technical Knowledge

Understanding Your Home Heating System

Pronto Air's original website explains several common residential heating systems and how they provide comfort throughout the home.

01 — Furnaces

Furnaces

Central furnaces distribute warm air through a home's ductwork. According to the original website, these systems can operate using electricity or natural gas.

Electric or Natural Gas fuel options available in central setups.
02 — Heat Pumps

Heat Pumps

Heat pumps provide both heating and cooling. They transfer heat to help maintain indoor temperatures.

Versatile dual-season operation for year-round indoor climate balance.
03 — Air Handlers

Air Handlers

Air handlers contain components that move air through a home's duct system, supporting heating in winter and cooling during summer.

Essential for moving conditioned air efficiently through home ductwork.
