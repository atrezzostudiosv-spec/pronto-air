Our Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about air conditioning, heating systems, energy efficiency, and indoor air quality.

01. What's the difference between a new air conditioner and my older one?
Pronto Air explains that newer air conditioning systems are generally more energy-efficient than older systems and can use less energy, helping homeowners reduce energy costs.
02. What is Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio and how does it affect my power usage?
Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio, or SEER, measures seasonal cooling efficiency. Pronto Air explains that higher SEER ratings indicate greater efficiency and can help reduce energy usage.
03. How long should my A/C and heating unit last?
The lifespan depends on the equipment and its maintenance. According to Pronto Air, units that are properly maintained and regularly serviced can last up to 15 years.
04. How does Indoor Air Quality help me?
Pronto Air highlights the importance of indoor air quality for households concerned about airborne irritants, allergies, asthma, and irritation affecting the skin, eyes, nose, or throat. The company also mentions air quality products intended to help improve indoor air conditions.
05. What can an air filtration system do?
Pronto Air describes air filtration systems as helping remove unwanted particles and contaminants from indoor air, including pollen, dust, odors, and tobacco smoke. The company highlights air filtration as a consideration for households concerned about asthma or severe allergies.
